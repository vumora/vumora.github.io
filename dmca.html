DMCA / Take Down

Vumora does not host video files. Every player is a standard YouTube iframe. If you own a channel and want a video hidden from this site’s feed, send a removal request. We will stop listing that video ID in our local feed.

How to request removal

  1. Your name and contact email
  2. YouTube video URL or 11-character video ID
  3. Proof you are the rights holder (channel URL)
  4. A statement that the request is made in good faith

Email this information to the operator of this copy of Vumora dmca.vidloom@gmail.com

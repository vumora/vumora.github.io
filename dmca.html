DMCA / Content Take Down Policy

Vumora is an embed directory and does not host, store, or stream video media on its own servers. Every video plays directly from YouTube's servers via YouTube's official standard Iframe Player Embed API. All views, watch time, and advertising revenues accrue directly to the respective content creators and copyright owners on YouTube.

How to request feed exclusion

If you are a copyright owner or channel administrator and wish to have an embedded link to your video excluded from Vumora's discovery feed, please submit a request containing:

  1. Your full name, organizational title, and official contact email address.
  2. The specific YouTube Video URL or 11-character Video ID.
  3. Verification that you are the authorized copyright holder or authorized representative (e.g. YouTube channel link).
  4. A statement made in good faith that the listing is requested to be excluded.

Please send your notice to our designated copyright contact:
vumora.official@gmail.com

We review and process legitimate exclusion requests within 48 business hours.

✨ Vumora Features

Vumora is a free, simple video explorer. Every video plays through the official YouTube player, so views and earnings stay with the creators.

  • 📍

    Local home feed

    The home feed follows your country automatically. You can switch between 64 regions any time in Settings.

  • 🔎

    Fast search

    Type any topic, song, team or creator and get results right away.

  • 🎬

    Shorts and Videos

    Filter the feed by short clips or full-length videos. Shorts open in a full-screen, swipe-style reel.

  • 🗂️

    A to Z categories

    Browse 26 topic chips, from Autos to Zoo, plus a For You shortcut.

  • 📺

    Mini player

    Keep watching while you scroll. Switch between a small player and a big screen whenever you like.

  • 💬

    Share and open

    Share any video on WhatsApp, or open it on YouTube with one tap.

  • 🌐

    28 languages, light and dark themes

    Change the language and the theme from Settings to read comfortably.

  • 📲

    Install as an app

    Add Vumora to your home screen for quick access. Pull down to refresh and scroll on for more videos.

  • ⭐

    Featured videos

    Any visitor can feature up to 2 YouTube videos per day from Settings. A featured video appears in the feed for 24 hours with a Featured label.

    Shopping guides

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026. Operator: Pawan Verma / PAWANGAMINGSTUDIO.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Vumora does not require account creation, registration, passwords, phone numbers, or credit card details. We do not store or sell your personal data.

2. On-Device Storage (LocalStorage & Cache)

We use your browser's local storage solely to retain your UI preferences (light/dark theme, language choice, mini-player placement, and local seen-video history). This data never leaves your device.

3. Geolocation & Feed Personalization

To present trending video content relevant to your country, client-side IP lookup requests may be made to public geo-resolution services (such as get.geojs.io, ipwho.is, or api.country.is). Vumora does not store or log your IP address on any central database.

4. Embedded YouTube Player & Google Services

Videos on Vumora are delivered via the official YouTube-nocookie embedded player. When you interact with the player, Google/YouTube policies apply. For details, refer to Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Amazon Associates Program & Affiliate Cookies

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Vumora is a participant in the Amazon Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn commission by advertising and linking to Amazon.in (Tag: vumora-21).

When you click on an external link pointing to Amazon.in, Amazon may place session cookies on your device in accordance with Amazon's Privacy Policy to track qualifying referrals. No personal information is accessed or stored by Vumora during this process.

6. Compliance with Indian DPDP Act, 2023

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023 of India, Vumora does not process or monetize personal identification information of its users.

7. Grievance Redressal & Contact

For any inquiries regarding data protection or site policies, contact our Grievance Officer at: vumora.official@gmail.com

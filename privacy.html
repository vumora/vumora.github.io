Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026. Operator: Pawan Verma / PAWANGAMINGSTUDIO.

VidLoom does not require an account. We do not sell your data.

On your device: theme and language may be saved in localStorage. A service worker may cache the app shell (HTML/CSS/JS/icons) so the PWA can open faster. Video files are not stored.

Approximate location: the home feed may call a public geo lookup (country/city from IP) so results can match your region. We do not run our own user database.

Third parties: YouTube / Google receive data when a video plays in their iframe (their privacy policy applies). Search/listing may use public Invidious instances. Thumbnails load from YouTube image servers.
